# Interface assets

`public/assets/openai.svg` and `public/assets/claude.svg` identify account providers. Sources are [Lobe Icons OpenAI](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/openai.svg) and [Lobe Icons Claude](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/claude-color.svg). The package's MIT copyright/license notice is retained in `public/assets/lobe-icons-LICENSE.txt`; trademarks remain with their owners. No account credentials or personal data are included in bundled assets. Fonts use local OS fallbacks.
